Ahmedabad Metro App - Live Route Planner, Map & Timings

Plan your journey across the city with the ultimate Ahmedabad Metro app. Whether you are a daily commuter, a student, or a tourist exploring Gujarat, AhmMetro is your all-in-one companion for navigating the Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar metro network. Get real-time train tracking, exact arrival times, ticket fares, and the fastest routes instantly—even when you do not have internet access. 

KEY FEATURES
- Live Route Planner: Simply enter your starting station and destination. The app instantly calculates the fastest route, total travel time, and exact ticket fare. 
- Real-Time Train Tracking: Know exactly when your train will arrive with live timings for every station. 
- Complete Offline Support: The routing engine, fare calculator, and timings work 100% offline, even when you lose signal underground. 
- Interactive Metro Map: View the entire Ahmedabad Metro network in a clean, high-quality interactive map that is easy to zoom and navigate. 
- Interchange Guidance: Step-by-step instructions on where to change trains at major junctions like Old High Court, Koteshwar Road, and GNLU. 
- Station Information: Discover all 53 stations across Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar. 

COMPLETE LINE COVERAGE
AhmMetro is fully updated with the newest operational lines and extensions:

- Red Line: APMC to Koteshwar Road (North-South Corridor)
