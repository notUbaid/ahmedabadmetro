Ahmedabad Metro Lines

Ahmedabad Metro (Gujarat Metro / MEGA Metro) operates 4 metro lines with 45+ stations:

Blue Line (East–West): Thaltej Gam to Vastral Gam

18 stations, ~45 minutes. Stations: Thaltej Gam, Thaltej, Doordarshan Kendra, Gurukul Road, Gujarat University, Commerce Six Road, S P Stadium, Old High Court (interchange), Shahpur, Gheekanta, Kalupur, Kankaria East, Apparel Park, Amraiwadi, Rabari Colony, Vastral, Nirant Cross Roads, Vastral Gam.

Red Line (North–South): APMC to Koteshwar Road

15 stations, ~33 minutes. Stations: APMC, Jivraj Park, Rajiv Nagar, Shreyas, Paldi, Gandhigram, Old High Court (interchange), Usmanpura, Vijay Nagar, Vadaj, Ranip, AEC, Sabarmati, Motera Stadium, Koteshwar Road (interchange).

Green Line (Gandhinagar Extension): Koteshwar Road to Mahatma Mandir

20 stations, ~55 minutes. Stations: Koteshwar Road (interchange), Vishwakarma College, Tapovan Circle, Narmada Canal, Koba Circle, Juna Koba, Koba Gam, GNLU (interchange), Raysan, Randesan, Dholakuva Circle, Infocity, Sector-1, Sector-10A, Sachivalaya, Akshardham, Juna Sachivalaya, Sector 16, Sector 24, Mahatma Mandir.

Purple Line (GIFT City): GNLU to GIFT City

3 stations, ~6 minutes. Stations: GNLU (interchange), PDPU, GIFT City.

Ahmedabad Metro Timing

Ahmedabad Metro operates daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Peak hours (8 AM–11 AM, 5 PM–8 PM): every 5–7 minutes. Off-peak: every 10–15 minutes. First train: 6:00 AM. Last train: ~9:15 PM from terminal stations.

Ahmedabad Metro Fare

Fares range from ₹5 to ₹30 based on distance. Metro Smart Card offers discounted fares.

Interchange Stations

Old High Court: Blue Line ↔ Red Line. Koteshwar Road: Red Line ↔ Green Line. GNLU: Green Line ↔ Purple Line.
