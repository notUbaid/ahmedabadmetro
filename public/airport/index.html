How to Reach Ahmedabad Airport by Metro (2026)

Verified guide for air travelers traveling to or from Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport (AMD / SVPIA). Because direct airport metro tracks are currently under construction as part of GMRC Phase 2A, air travelers connect via the nearest operational Red/Green Line stations and last-mile feeder options.

🗺️ Recommended Metro Routes to Airport

From Kalupur Railway Station
Blue Line → Red Line
Board Blue Line at Kalupur, switch at Old High Court to Red Line, alight at Koteshwar Road (6.8 km feeder to Airport). Or board direct AMTS Bus 15 outside station.
From Thaltej / SG Highway
Blue Line → Red Line
Board at Thaltej, switch at Old High Court to northbound Red Line, alight at Koteshwar Road. Total ~30 min metro ride + short auto to airport.
From Gandhinagar / GIFT City
Green Line Direct
Take Green Line directly south towards Koteshwar Road. Quickest connection for Gandhinagar and Secretariat flyers.

🏗️ GMRC Phase 2A Airport Metro Status

To provide seamless rapid rail transit directly into the flight terminals, Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC) has designed Phase 2A: a dedicated 6 km spur corridor extending from the main corridor directly to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport. The alignment includes 4 elevated stations and an underground station situated adjacent to the airport terminal building. Construction is currently underway.

❓ Airport Metro Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a direct metro station inside Ahmedabad Airport (SVPIA)?
No. As of 2026, there is no operational metro station inside Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport (SVPIA). A dedicated 6 km airport spur corridor (Phase 2A) with 4 elevated stations and 1 underground airport terminal station is currently under construction by GMRC.
Which is the nearest operational metro station to Ahmedabad Airport?
The closest operational metro station to Ahmedabad Airport is Koteshwar Road Metro Station (6.8 km away), followed by Motera Stadium Metro Station (7.5 km away) on the Red/Green corridor. From either station, commuters take a metered auto-rickshaw or app-based cab (Uber/Ola) to reach Terminal 1 (Domestic) or Terminal 2 (International) in approximately 12 to 18 minutes.
How do auto-rickshaws and cabs operate from the nearest metro station to the airport?
Auto-rickshaws in Ahmedabad operate on digital distance meters or fixed app pricing (Uber Auto / Ola Auto). Ride-hailing cabs (Uber, Ola, and BluSmart) can be booked directly for pickup at the station exit gates. Inside airport arrival terminals, pre-paid taxi counters and dedicated app-cab pickup zones are available.
What are the luggage rules for air travelers taking Ahmedabad Metro?
Air travelers may carry 1 standard baggage item weighing up to 25 kg with maximum dimensions of 80 cm x 50 cm x 30 cm free of charge per passenger. Oversized commercial parcels or hazardous cargo are strictly prohibited.
Can I use the metro for early morning flights from Ahmedabad Airport?
Ahmedabad Metro begins morning train operations at 06:16 AM (Red Line) and 06:20 AM (Blue Line). For early morning flights requiring check-in before 07:30 AM, taking the metro is not feasible; travelers should book a direct taxi or cab.
