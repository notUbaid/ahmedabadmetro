❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the metro cost from Sachivalaya to Vadaj?
A standard paper token costs ₹30. If you pay using a GMRC Smart Card or NCMC card, you receive an automatic 10% discount, making the total fare ₹27.
How long is the travel time from Sachivalaya to Vadaj?
The expected journey time is approximately 43 minutes across 19 stops covering roughly 24.7 km. Travel times may vary slightly during peak rush hours.
Which stations come between Sachivalaya and Vadaj?
The stations on this route in order are: Sachivalaya, Sector-10A, Sector-1, Infocity, Dholakuva Circle, Randesan, Raysan, GNLU, Koba Gam, Juna Koba, Koba Circle, Narmada Canal, Tapovan Circle, Vishwakarma College, Koteshwar Road, Motera Stadium, Sabarmati, AEC, Ranip, Vadaj.
Do I need to change trains between Sachivalaya and Vadaj?
Yes, you must transfer trains at Koteshwar Road. You do not need to exit the fare gates or buy a new ticket.
Which platform and train direction should I board at Sachivalaya?
1. At Sachivalaya, board the Green Line (Gandhinagar Corridor) train heading towards Koteshwar Road.
2. Alight at Koteshwar Road Interchange and transfer between Red Line and Green Line.
3. Board the Red Line (North-South) train heading towards APMC and alight at Vadaj.
What is the luggage allowance on Ahmedabad Metro?
Passengers are permitted up to 15 kg of personal baggage free of charge, with maximum dimensions not exceeding 60 cm x 45 cm x 25 cm. Baggage scanners and security screening are active at all station entry points.
