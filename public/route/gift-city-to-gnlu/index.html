❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the metro cost from GIFT City to GNLU?
A standard paper token costs ₹10. If you pay using a GMRC Smart Card or NCMC card, you receive an automatic 10% discount, making the total fare ₹9.
How long is the travel time from GIFT City to GNLU?
The expected journey time is approximately 4 minutes across 2 stops covering roughly 2.6 km. Travel times may vary slightly during peak rush hours.
Which stations come between GIFT City and GNLU?
The stations on this route in order are: GIFT City, PDEU, GNLU.
Do I need to change trains between GIFT City and GNLU?
No, this is a direct metro route. You can remain on the same train from start to finish.
Which platform and train direction should I board at GIFT City?
At GIFT City, head to the platform and board the Purple Line (GIFT City Branch) train heading towards GNLU. Travel 2 stops and alight at GNLU.
What is the luggage allowance on Ahmedabad Metro?
Passengers are permitted up to 15 kg of personal baggage free of charge, with maximum dimensions not exceeding 60 cm x 45 cm x 25 cm. Baggage scanners and security screening are active at all station entry points.
