Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 12, 2026

Government Disclaimer: This application does not represent a government entity. AhmMetro is an independent, privately-developed utility application. All metro timings, routes, and fare information provided in this app are sourced directly from the official Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC). This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to GMRC or the Government of Gujarat in any official capacity.

1. Information We Collect

AhmMetro ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. Our application does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to our servers.

2. Location Data

If you choose to use the location-based features (such as finding the nearest metro station), the app requests access to your device's location. This location data is processed locally on your device and is strictly used to provide routing services. We do not store or track your location history on any external servers.

3. Offline Storage

The app uses local browser storage (such as IndexedDB and Service Workers) to cache route timings and map data so the app can function offline. This data remains on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

We use OpenStreetMap for rendering map tiles. When loading map tiles, your IP address may be visible to the tile providers in accordance with their standard web logging policies.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the app.
