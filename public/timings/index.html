❓ Frequently Asked Questions about Ahmedabad Metro Timings

What are the first and last train timings for Ahmedabad Metro?
On the Blue Line (Thaltej Gam ⇄ Vastral Gam) and Red Line (APMC ⇄ Koteshwar Road), operations start at 06:20 AM and 06:16 AM respectively, and close with the final departure at 10:00 PM daily. The Green Line operates from 07:33 AM to 08:10 PM.
Does Ahmedabad Metro run on Sundays and public holidays?
Yes, Ahmedabad Metro runs 365 days a year. On Sundays and national holidays, morning operations begin at 07:00 AM with consistent 10–12 minute train frequencies throughout the afternoon and evening.
What is the train frequency during peak office hours?
During peak morning rush (08:00 AM to 11:00 AM) and evening rush (05:00 PM to 08:00 PM), trains arrive every 5 to 7 minutes on Blue and Red Lines. Off-peak midday headway is 10 to 12 minutes.
What are the metro timings for GIFT City?
The Purple Line connects GNLU to GIFT City during tech park office shifts in two windows: morning service runs from 07:36 AM to 10:18 AM and evening service runs from 04:06 PM to 07:15 PM.
What are the metro timings from Ahmedabad Junction (Kalupur) railway station?
At Kalupur Railway Station, the first westbound train toward Thaltej Gam departs at 06:30 AM, while the first eastbound train toward Vastral Gam departs at 06:32 AM. The last train in both directions departs at approximately 10:15 PM.
Are metro operating hours extended during cricket matches at Narendra Modi Stadium?
Yes! For IPL matches and international cricket games at Narendra Modi Stadium (Motera Stadium station), GMRC operates special post-match trains until 12:00 AM (midnight) or later, running every 3 to 5 minutes to disperse stadium crowds safely.
