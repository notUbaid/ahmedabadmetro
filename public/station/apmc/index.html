❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are the first and last train timings at APMC?
The first metro train departs from APMC at 06:20 AM on weekdays and 06:20 AM on Sundays. The last train departs at 11:33 PM on weekdays and 06:20 AM on Sundays.
What is the metro ticket fare from APMC?
Official GMRC ticket fares from APMC range from ₹5 for the shortest distance up to ₹30 for maximum distance journeys. Passengers paying with a GMRC Smart Card or RuPay NCMC card receive an automatic 10% discount on each ride.
Which lines and connections are available at APMC?
APMC is on the Red Line (North-South Corridor). It connects seamlessly to city bus (AMTS/BRTS) and local transit hubs.
What facilities are available at APMC Metro Station?
APMC is equipped with lifts and escalators for accessibility, automatic ticket vending machines (TVM), security checkpoints, clean drinking water, accessible restrooms, and customer service desks.
