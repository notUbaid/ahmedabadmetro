❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many metro stations are there in Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar?
There are currently 53 operational metro stations across Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, and GIFT City across 4 lines (Blue, Red, Green, and Purple).
Which metro stations are underground in Ahmedabad?
There are 4 underground metro stations on the Blue Line in Ahmedabad: Shahpur, Gheekanta, Kalupur (Railway Station), and Kankaria East. All other 49 stations are elevated.
What are the interchange stations on the Ahmedabad Metro?
The 3 official interchange stations are: 1. Old High Court (Blue Line ⇄ Red Line), 2. Koteshwar Road (Red Line ⇄ Green Line), and 3. GNLU (Green Line ⇄ Purple Line GIFT City branch).
