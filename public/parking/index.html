Ahmedabad Metro Parking Guide (2026)

Everything commuters need to know about vehicle parking across the Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC) network. Because Ahmedabad Metro operates on elevated viaducts along road medians, parking is managed in coordination with the Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC) through dedicated plots and municipal Pay & Park facilities at major transit hubs.

Parking Availability
Selected Hubs Only
Dedicated plots at major terminus & railway junction stations; limited on-street bays at minor stops.
Operating Authority
AMC & Contractors
Managed by Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC) and appointed local parking operators.
Smart Card Payment
Not Integrated
Metro Smart Cards cannot be used for parking. Payments are made via cash, UPI, or municipal slip.
Best Park & Ride
Terminus Stations
Vastral Gam, Thaltej Gam, APMC, Motera Stadium, Ranip, and Sabarmati.

⚠️ Essential Parking Rules & Towing Warnings

No Parking Under Viaducts
Strict Tow-Away Zones
Parking along road medians or BRTS corridors under metro pillars without signage is strictly prohibited by Ahmedabad Traffic Police.
Use Authorized Lots
Insist on Receipt
Always park in designated AMC Pay & Park areas where authorized attendants provide a valid printed or digital slip.
Helmet & Valuables
Commuter Responsibility
Do not leave bags, electronics, or unattended helmets on vehicles. Lock two-wheelers securely in authorized bays.
Overnight Commuters
Use Railway Hubs
Standard roadside metro parking bays are not guarded overnight. Use 24/7 parking at Kalupur or Sabarmati railway stations for multi-day trips.

❓ Parking Frequently Asked Questions

Do all Ahmedabad Metro stations have dedicated parking facilities?
No. GMRC stations are elevated structures constructed on road medians, so GMRC does not operate standardized metro-owned parking complexes at every station. Instead, commuter parking is provided through designated Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC) plots at major terminal hubs (such as Vastral Gam, Thaltej Gam, APMC, Vadaj, and Sabarmati), municipal Pay & Park sites, and on-street designated parking bays.
Can I pay for metro parking using the GMRC Metro Smart Card?
No. GMRC Metro Smart Cards and RuPay NCMC transit cards are currently valid only for automatic fare collection (AFC) turnstiles on train platforms. Parking lots are managed independently by the Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC) or authorized local contractors, where charges are collected via cash, UPI, or municipal parking receipts.
Which Ahmedabad Metro stations are best for Park & Ride?
The best stations for Park & Ride are terminal and major multi-modal transit stations that have dedicated surface parking plots: Vastral Gam (East terminus), Thaltej Gam / Thaltej (West terminus), APMC Vasna (South terminus), Motera Stadium, Ranip (GSRTC Bus Terminal), and Sabarmati / AEC (Railway Junction). Intermediate high-density stations like Old High Court or Commerce Six Road have very limited parking.
Is overnight parking permitted at Ahmedabad Metro stations?
Overnight parking is not available at standard elevated metro stations. For overnight or long-duration vehicle parking, commuters are advised to use 24-hour guarded municipal or railway parking complexes, such as Ahmedabad Junction (Kalupur) or Sabarmati Railway Station.
