❓ Interchange Frequently Asked Questions

How do I transfer between the Blue Line and Red Line at Old High Court?
Old High Court is a 2-level stacked interchange station. Blue Line (East-West) runs on Level 1, while Red Line (North-South) runs on Level 2. Commuters simply take the escalators or elevators between levels inside the paid fare concourse without passing through exit gates.
Do I need to buy two tickets to travel on both metro lines?
No. A single token or Metro Smart Card covers your entire journey across all connected lines. The fare is calculated automatically based on total stations traveled, with zero extra surcharge for transferring lines.
How long does an interchange transfer take at Old High Court?
Walking between platforms takes approximately 2 to 3 minutes. Dual-speed escalators and wide elevators provide rapid, barrier-free access between levels.
Where is the Gandhinagar and GIFT City interchange?
Transfer from Ahmedabad Red Line to Gandhinagar Green Line occurs at Koteshwar Road Metro Station. Transfer to the Purple Line (GIFT City branch shuttle) takes place at GNLU Metro Station.
